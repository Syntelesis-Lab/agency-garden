# Roadmap

The long-term vision is an open laboratory for artificial agency. New capabilities should make important questions investigable while preserving understandable evidence.

| Horizon | Capability | Acceptance direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0.1 alpha | Finite worlds, controlled conditions and durable evidence | Install, run, inspect and extend outside the repository |
| Continuing projects | Histories, artifacts, undertakings and relationships | Two applications reuse contracts; branches stay isolated |
| Institutions | Ratification, authority, dissent, amendment and withdrawal | Distinguish enforced restrictions from voluntary adherence |
| Persistent worlds | Longer histories, configurable memory/learning and proposed activities | Measure continuity, cost and scale; identify interventions |

Welfare inquiry informs every horizon. Each study should identify its empirical premise and the theoretical bridge to an agent's own good. A generic welfare score or consciousness verdict is not a scheduled feature.

Promote prototypes after a second concrete application shows what should be shared. Research can use small dedicated extensions while APIs mature. Near-term priorities are external first-use feedback, complete research applications, evidence navigation and a carefully designed history/branching interface. Later dates are not promised.
