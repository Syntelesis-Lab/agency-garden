# Agency Garden artwork

The hero illustration was generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool and refined with Laurentiu Meirosu. The selected composition shows separate floating worlds for creative work, shared deliberation and a collaborative building project. It illustrates the project's mission; it is not a screenshot of the software.

The original reference direction was replaced by an original Agency Garden composition. The final asset is `agency-garden.png`. The final editing prompt is retained below.

## Final prompt

Rework this Agency Garden illustration into a more GAME-LIKE ISOMETRIC WORLD OF SEPARATE FLOATING ISLANDS. Preserve its meaningful subjects, warm golden light, emerald/teal and ochre colors, painterly charm and sense of wonder, but simplify the dense garden. Exactly THREE clearly separated floating terrain islands with visible thick stone-and-earth cross sections and generous sky between them: left island has one small open workshop where a tiny stylized game-character participant makes an original object; larger center island has a simple round meeting table with four varied game-character participants arranging physical pieces together, one tree, some lawn and two branching paths; right island has an unfinished bridge/project where two participants are building together and a path where another chooses their own direction. A single simple partial footbridge may connect two islands, but keep the islands visibly distinct and suspended in an airy sky. This is a world of purposes, deliberation, relationships, collaboration and choices; do NOT turn it into generic farm scenery. Simplify buildings to clean readable game-environment shapes, make the characters small and approachable with clear silhouettes, reduce ornamental props, dense vines, shelves, banners, distant structures and flowers. A few coherent shrubs, stones and flowers provide warmth. Camera: elevated consistent isometric game-board perspective, pulled back enough to show all three islands entirely. Render as polished hand-painted cozy strategy/adventure game concept art, not photorealistic and not a flat vector diagram. Broad sculpted shapes, tasteful detail, easy to read at README size. Background blue/teal sky with a few soft clouds fading toward warm ivory below. Wide panoramic roughly 2.4:1 banner composition. No typography, no text, no logos, no watermark, no HUD, no hearts, no robots, no barns or livestock. Retain the original artwork's philosophical meaning while making the world simpler, playful, separated and floating.
